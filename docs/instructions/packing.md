# 🎒 Packing List — Euro Trip 2026

**4 people · 4 carry-ons · 18 days · Aug 19 → Sep 5**
Porto → Lisbon → Barcelona → Zaragoza → Madrid → Segovia

> **The rule:** we pack **6 days of clothes and do laundry twice**. Everything else gets bought at the first supermarket in Porto. Nothing is packed "just in case" — Portugal and Spain have pharmacies, Mercadona and Decathlon on every other corner.

---

## ⚖️ The Hard Constraint

**TAP Discount Economy = 0 checked bags on all 4 TAP segments.** The binding limit is the cabin bag, and TAP weighs it at the gate at OPO and LIS.

- **Cabin bag:** 1 per person · **max 8 kg** · **55 × 40 × 20 cm**
- **Personal item:** 1 per person · **40 × 30 × 15 cm** (small backpack, tote, laptop bag)
- **AA95 MAD→JFK (Sep 5):** 56 × 36 × 23 cm, **no weight limit** + personal item

> ⚠️ **Verify both before packing:** [flytap.com baggage](https://www.flytap.com/en-us/baggage/hand-luggage) and aa.com. Fare rules move.

> 💡 **The bag itself eats the allowance.** A hard-shell 40 L spinner weighs **2.5–3 kg empty** — over a third of the 8 kg budget gone before a single sock. Use a **soft carry-on or travel backpack under 1.5 kg**. This single choice is worth more than any packing trick.

**Realistic weight budget per person:**

- Bag: 1.2 kg
- Clothes: 3.5 kg
- Shoes (the pair *not* worn): 0.6 kg
- Toiletries: 0.8 kg
- Electronics + cables: 0.9 kg
- Slack: 1.0 kg → **~8.0 kg**

Anything heavy (jeans, books, hair tools, second jacket) blows this. Weigh each bag at home **before** Aug 19.

---

## 🌡️ What the Weather Actually Requires

- **Porto** Aug 20–23 — 25–28 °C, Atlantic breeze, **cool evenings (18–20 °C)**, sailboat cruise Aug 21 is windy on the water, small chance of drizzle
- **Lisbon** Aug 23–26 — 28–32 °C, hilly, slippery *calçada* cobbles
- **Barcelona** Aug 26–31 — 28–31 °C and **humid**, sea 24 °C, beach Aug 29
- **Montserrat** Aug 28 — 1,200 m mountain, noticeably cooler and windy, lots of walking
- **Zaragoza** Aug 31 – Sep 1 — hottest stop, **33–36 °C, bone dry**
- **Madrid** Sep 1–5 — 26–32 °C day, **16–19 °C at night**
- **Segovia** Sep 4 — 1,000 m altitude, cooler than Madrid, exposed aqueduct plaza

**Translation:** hot days everywhere, but **every single person needs one real layer** for Porto evenings, Montserrat, and Madrid nights. One layer. Not two.

- [ ] 1 × sleepwear, light

- [ ] Reusable water bottle (empty through security, refill everywhere)

**That's it.** Five tops for eighteen days works because we wash twice. It does not work if you pack six pairs of jeans "to be safe".

- [ ] Big water bottles for the apartments

## 🏠 Sep 5 — The Way Home (Read This Before Buying Souvenirs)

> ⚠️ **Port wine, olive oil, vermouth and anything else liquid over 100 ml CANNOT go in a carry-on.** With 0 checked bags, every bottle bought at Bolhão or Mercadona is a bottle you cannot fly home with.

Three ways around it:

1. **Buy airside at MAD on Sep 5.** Duty-free liquids in a sealed STEB bag are fine, and JFK is the final stop — no connection to break the seal.
2. **Check a bag on AA95.** ⚠️ **Verify the fare on aa.com** — American Main Cabin transatlantic normally includes a free checked bag; Basic Economy does not. If it's included, that's the souvenir route home, and worth confirming *before* the trip so you can shop freely.
3. **Ship it.** CTT (Portugal) and Correos (Spain) both handle wine; expensive and slow.

**Departure morning checklist:**

- [ ] Fare checked — free checked bag on AA95, yes or no
- [ ] Nothing liquid over 100 ml in a cabin bag
- [ ] Foldable tote deployed as the 5th bag if needed
- [ ] Every apartment swept: chargers in wall sockets, bathroom shelves, under beds, in the safe
- [ ] Keys returned per each host's instructions
