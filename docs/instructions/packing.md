**4 people · 4 carry-ons + 2 checked bags · 18 days · Aug 19 → Sep 5**
Porto → Lisbon → Barcelona → Zaragoza → Madrid (~~→ Segovia~~ dropped Aug 5)

> **The model:** the **4 carry-ons are the trip** — that's what you live out of, in and out daily. The **2 checked bags are freight** — bulk, liquids, and everything heading to Emily in Madrid. They get opened at each apartment for refills and otherwise stay shut.

## 🌡️ What the Weather Actually Requires

- **Porto** Aug 20–23 — 25–28 °C, Atlantic breeze, **cool evenings (18–20 °C)**, the Aug 21 sailboat is windy on the water, small chance of drizzle
- **Lisbon** Aug 23–26 — 28–32 °C, hilly, slippery *calçada* cobbles
- **Barcelona** Aug 26–31 — 28–31 °C and **humid**, sea 24 °C, beach Aug 29
- **Montserrat** Aug 28 — 1,200 m mountain, noticeably cooler and windy, lots of walking
- **Zaragoza** Aug 31 – Sep 1 — hottest stop, **33–36 °C, bone dry**
- **Madrid** Sep 1–5 — 26–32 °C day, **16–19 °C at night**
- ~~**Segovia** Sep 4~~ — ❌ dropped Aug 5 (Emily's UAM day). Sep 4 is now Madrid + **El Pardo** — same Madrid weather, but the Monte de El Pardo is open woodland: hats and water for the midday walk

**Translation:** hot days everywhere, but **every person needs one real layer** for Porto evenings, Montserrat, and Madrid nights.

## 🚉 Aug 31 – Sep 1 — The Two Train Days

**This is the hard part of the trip, not the packing.** After Barcelona there are no more flights: 4 people move 4 carry-ons and 2 full-size suitcases through two AVE trains, two apartments and two locker runs.

**Renfe rules:** 3 items per passenger, 25 kg total each, max 85 × 55 × 35 cm per bag. Four passengers, so allowance is not the issue — **rack space is**. Both Sants and Atocha have airport-style X-ray security.

- [ ] **Aug 31 — be at Barcelona-Sants by 10:30** for the 11:00 (Coche 6). With 6 bags through security, do not shave this
- [ ] Board early and put the big cases in the **end-of-carriage luggage rack**, not overhead
- [ ] ⚠️ **Zaragoza, Aug 31:** train lands **12:28**, apartment check-in is **16:00** → ~3.5 hours with all 6 bags. Lockers at the **Delicias bus station** (same building, ~€5–8) — **confirm they have large lockers**, or message Isabel about an early bag drop
- [ ] ⚠️ **Zaragoza apartment is 2º Izda with stairs.** Two 23 kg cases up two flights, twice
- [ ] ⚠️ **Sep 1:** check-out **12:00**, train **17:48** → lockers again for ~5.5 hours. Ask Isabel first — a bag hold beats two locker runs
- [ ] **Sep 1 — be at Delicias by 17:15** (Coche 7)
- [ ] Madrid Atocha **19:44**, then taxi to the hotel — do not attempt the Metro with 6 bags at dinner hour

> ⚠️ **Check the Lisbon apartment before Aug 23.** It's **64 Rua da Bempostinha, 5º direito — fifth floor** [📍](https://maps.google.com/?q=Rua+da+Bempostinha+64+1150-099+Lisboa+Portugal). Ask the host directly whether there is a lift. If not, that's two full suitcases up five flights of Lisbon stairs, and it changes what you put in them.
