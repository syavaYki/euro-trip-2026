## 🍷 The one real gap: no port lodge

**There was no port-house visit anywhere in the three Porto days** — the single most Porto thing on offer, and it never landed. It was pencilled in as a Day-1 swap and lost to the Gaia bridge loop; on Day 3 the lodges sit on the wrong side of the river for a walk that had to end at the hotel.

⚠️ **Gaia lodges shut ~18:00–19:00 and you leave the flat at 06:15 on Sunday — this is a next-trip item now.**

| Lodge | What you get | Price |
| ----- | -------------- | ----- |
| **[Cálem](https://maps.google.com/?q=Calem+Porto+Portugal)** [🌐](https://www.calem.pt) | The default first-timer's lodge, 2 min from where the bridge's lower deck lands. Tour + 2-wine tasting, sensory barrel museum, a "Tour, Taste & Fado" version | ~€22–28 adult · youth 6–17 reduced, no tasting · **timed slots, book ahead** |
| **[Graham's](https://maps.google.com/?q=Graham%27s+Port+Lodge+Vila+Nova+de+Gaia+Portugal)** | The hillside one with the view and the serious cellar; Vintage Room tastings at the top end | up to ~€70 |
| **Quevedo** | The small family house — the cheap, unstuffy way in | from ~€12 |
| **Ferreira / Ramos Pinto / Cockburn's / Taylor's** | All within a few minutes of each other on the Gaia quay | €15–30 typical |

> 💡 Emily is 18 and can legally taste in Portugal; a minor gets the youth rate and juice. **[Half Rabbit by Bordalo II](https://maps.google.com/?q=%22Half+Rabbit%22+by+Bordalo+II+Porto+Portugal)** is 3 min from Cálem — fold it in.

## 🧾 Field notes & corrections

Things learned on the ground or while checking, worth keeping straight.

| Note | Detail |
| ---- | ------ |
| **Tram 1 tickets = 30% off the Tram Museum** ✅ | Confirmed at the desk **Sat Aug 22, 2026**. Unadvertised, and nobody asks — hand the tram tickets over unprompted. €24 of entry became ~€16.80. **It runs both ways:** the museum ticket includes one free tram/metro/bus ride within 4 hrs |
| **Tram fares disagree across sources** | Tram 1 is quoted **€6 single** (our figure, and what's in the master) but some sources say €3.50 single / €6 for two trips / €10 for a 2-day pass. Tram 22 is quoted €3.50 with a ~€7 24-hour historic pass. **Buy on board and don't budget to the cent** |
| **Bus 500 is the cheap way to Foz** | Praça da Liberdade / São Bento → Passeio Alegre → Matosinhos Mercado, every ~20 min, riverside road the whole way. **Z2 €1.40** on the Andante against €6 for the tram |
| **Tram 1 service window** | First from Infante 09:00; **last complete run out of Passeio Alegre 20:10** |
| **Casa de Pasto da Palmeira address** | Listings split between **R. do Passeio Alegre 294 and 450**. It's the one on the garden side. Phone +351 226 168 244 |
| **Igreja do Carmo circuit closes for Mass** | Published price drifts €5–7 (we used ~€7); summer hours 09:30–18:00, but it's a working parish and **Saturday evening Mass shuts the circuit without notice** |
| **Solar do Vinho do Porto is closed** | Checked Aug 22, 2026. The Lisbon Solar is a different, still-open venue — don't be misled by search results |
