### Getting Around

- **Airport → hotel (Aug 26 night):** taxi **~€35–40** (easiest at 22:00), or Aerobus A1 to Pl. Catalunya (**€7.45**/pp, runs past midnight) + short hop
- **Metro: 4 × T-casual (€12.55 each = €50.20)** — single-person, 10 rides each; covers Metro/bus/tram/FGC zone 1 + Montjuïc & Vallvidrera **funiculars** (never the €13 Montjuïc **cable car**). Shareable alternative: T-familiar €11.50/8 rides (no airport). **Skip Hola Barcelona** (96h = €35.60 pp ≈ €142 for four; needs ~28 rides pp, we'll do ~9–12)
- **Day trips:** Montserrat (**Fri Option A**) = FGC R5 from Pl. d'Espanya, 08:36 — the **Tot Montserrat** combo (~€71.50, full price from age 5) bundles Metro + train + cremallera/cable car + Sant Joan funicular + museum + buffet lunch. Girona (**now Sat**) = fast train from Sants, ~38 min, ~€8–16 pp each way. **Valencia (Sun) = hire car**, ~3h30–4h each way on the **toll-free** AP-7; Sunday parking in Valencia is free. ⚠️ Montserrat's **Santa Cova funicular is out of service** (path walkable)
- **Taxis/Bolt:** €6–12 across town; ~€10–12 hotel ↔ Park Güell or Sants
- **Weather:** 28–31 °C, humid; sea ~24 °C. **Sunset ~20:30** all stay

> ⚠️ **Pickpockets** — Las Ramblas, Gothic Quarter, airport Metro, Sagrada exit, the beach. Bags in front; phones off café tables. **Cash €60–80** for the old bodegas (La Cova Fumada is cash-only).
