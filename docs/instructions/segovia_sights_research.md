# Segovia Sights & Attractions
## Sep 4 (day trip from Madrid) · Euro Trip 2026

> Segovia is one of the most perfectly preserved medieval cities in Spain — a **Roman aqueduct that needs no cement**, a **fairy-tale Alcázar on a cliff**, the **last great Gothic cathedral** built anywhere in Spain, and an entire **medieval Jewish Quarter** preserved in stone. **UNESCO World Heritage since 1985**, ~27 min from Madrid by Avant, and almost magically untouristed in the late afternoon once the bus groups leave.
>
> Mix of **essential sights** (Aqueduct, Alcázar, Cathedral) and **hidden corners** (Templar Vera Cruz, the mirador below the Alcázar, the Sephardic cemetery) — do at least one from each category.

## 🏛️ Main Attractions

| Name | Why | Cost | Hours Fri Sep 4 | Book? | Tip |

| **Roman Aqueduct** [📍](https://maps.google.com/?q=Roman+Aqueduct+Segovia+Spain) ⭐⭐ | The most impressive Roman engineering left in Spain: **166 arches, 29 m high**, ~17,000 granite blocks, **no mortar, no cement, no clamps**. ## 🔍 Hidden Gems & Off-the-Beaten-Path

> Most day-trippers leave after Aqueduct + Alcázar + cathedral. The hidden corners are where Segovia turns from a checklist into a place.

| Name | What | Cost | Hours Fri Sep 4 | Tip |
|------|------|------|------------------|-----|
| **Iglesia de la Vera Cruz** [📍](https://maps.google.com/?q=Iglesia+de+la+Vera+Cruz+Segovia+Spain) ⭐⭐ | **12-sided Romanesque Templar church**, consecrated 1208 — one of Spain's only round-plan churches; its two-storey central edicule held a True Cross fragment | €2–3 cash | **10:30–13:30 + 16:00–19:00** (closed Mon; Tue opens 16:00) | Climb the tower for the best long view of the Alcázar across the valley — almost nobody makes it out here |
| **Mirador / Pradera de San Marcos** [📍](https://maps.google.com/?q=Pradera+de+San+Marcos+Segovia+Spain) ⭐⭐ | The **classic Segovia postcard** from the valley floor: the Alcázar's prow towering on the cliff directly above a picnic meadow | Free | Always | Ten minutes lying on the grass looking up = the family photo. Path down from the Alcázar gardens, 10–15 min |
| **Monasterio de El Parral** [📍](https://maps.google.com/?q=Monasterio+de+El+Parral+Segovia+Spain) ⭐ | 15th-century Hieronymite monastery founded by Enrique IV (Isabella's half-brother), **still inhabited by monks**; superb 15th-century retablo | Free (guided, donation) | **Guided passes Wed–Sun at 11:00 & 17:00 ONLY**; closed Mon–Tue | ⚠️ The oft-quoted "daily 19:00 Gregorian Vespers" doesn't hold up — the monks' own site lists **chant at Sunday 12:00 Mass** only. On our Friday: admire from the river path |
| **Cementerio Judío de El Pinarillo** [📍](https://maps.google.com/?q=Cementerio+Jud%C3%ADo+El+Pinarillo+Segovia+Spain) | Pre-1492 Sephardic cemetery — 100+ rock-cut graves on a pine hillside above the Eresma | Free | Always (gate may close at dusk) | 10-min uphill from Vera Cruz; silent, affecting — for the teens more than the parents |
| **Casa de los Picos** [📍](https://maps.google.com/?q=Casa+de+los+Picos+Segovia+Spain) | 15th-century palace faced with **617 carved granite pyramid spikes** | Free (courtyard) | Business hours (art school) | On the Calle Real walk-up — 5-min photo-and-courtyard stop, don't detour for it |
| **Casa de la Moneda** [📍](https://maps.google.com/?q=Casa+de+la+Moneda+Segovia+Spain) | **Spain's oldest royal mint** (1583, Felipe II) — water-powered coin hammers from the Tyrol, on the Eresma below the Alcázar | €3 | Tue–Sun ~10:00–14:00 + 16:00–20:00 — verify on site | The valley path passes its door; fascinating for older kids but skip if the day is running long |
| **Casa-Museo de Antonio Machado** [📍](https://maps.google.com/?q=Casa-Museo+de+Antonio+Machado+Segovia+Spain) | The preserved boarding-house room where poet Antonio Machado lived 1919–1932 | Free | Tue–Sun ~11:00–14:00 + 16:30–19:00 — verify on site | Tiny, moving, Spanish-language guided visit — only for a poetry-inclined return trip |
| **Puerta de San Andrés** [📍](https://maps.google.com/?q=Puerta+de+San+Andr%C3%A9s+Segovia+Spain) | Surviving medieval city gate at the Judería's south edge, free wall-interpretation room inside | Free | Tue–Sun ~10:30–14:00 + 16:00–18:00 | Best photo from outside the walls; on our Judería loop |
| **Sinagoga Mayor / Corpus Christi convent** [📍](https://maps.google.com/?q=Convento+del+Corpus+Christi+Segovia+Spain) | Segovia's main medieval synagogue, converted 1410, now a Poor Clares convent — the Mudéjar interior echoes Toledo's Santa María la Blanca | €1 | Mon/Wed/Thu 10:45–14:00 · Sat 10:45–14:00 + 16:00–18:00 · Sun 10:45–14:15 · ⚠️ **CLOSED Tue & Fri** | Closed on our day — see the doorway from Plaza del Corpus and keep walking |

## 🏘️ Neighbourhoods

| Area | Character | Do |
|------|-----------|-----|
| **Plaza del Azoguejo + Calle Real** | The Aqueduct's plaza, then the medieval main street (Cervantes → Juan Bravo) climbing into town | The natural arrival walk: Aqueduct → Casa de los Picos → Plaza San Martín → Plaza Mayor, 15 min uphill |
| **La Judería** | Lanes between cathedral and walls, pre-1492 street plan intact | Centro Didáctico by 12:45, then wander Vieja + Nueva to the Puerta de San Andrés |
| **Plaza Mayor** | Arcaded, proportioned, cathedral on one side, San Miguel (where Isabella was proclaimed queen) on another | The 19:00 ponche stop as the day-trip crowds thin |
| **Eresma valley** | Pine-shaded river floor below the Alcázar cliff — mint, monastery, meadow, Templars | The 17:20–18:40 loop: Pradera de San Marcos → Vera Cruz; quiet, green, ten degrees calmer |
