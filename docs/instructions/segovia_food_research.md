# Segovia Food & Drink
## Sep 4 (day trip from Madrid) · Euro Trip 2026

> Segovia has **one dish** and you eat it here or you've wasted the trip: **_cochinillo asado_** — whole roast suckling pig, skin crackling like glass, so tender it's carved with the **edge of a plate**. This is the dish's spiritual home — three rival restaurants have been doing it the same way for over a century each, and there is no better cochinillo in Spain than what's served beneath the Aqueduct. One big lunch, one pastry stop, home to Madrid for a light evening.

---

## 🐷 The Cochinillo Imperative

**_Cochinillo asado_** — also called _tostón_ locally — is Segovia's defining dish.

- **The pig:** 2–3 weeks old, exclusively milk-fed, **max ~4.5 kg dressed weight**, bred under the **D.O. Cochinillo de Segovia** at small farms in the surrounding countryside
- **The cooking:** slow-roasted in a **wood-fired clay oven** at 250–280°C for 90–120 minutes, basted with white wine and salted water, until the skin is so thin and crisp it **shatters like glass** and the meat needs no knife
- **The ceremony** (Cándido and Duque): the maître d' carves the pig at the table **with the edge of a ceramic plate** — proof of tenderness — then **smashes the plate on the floor**. Sharp crash, applause, wide-eyed kids
- **Portions:** minimum is usually a **_cuarto_ (quarter pig) per 2 diners** — for 4, order two cuartos or a _medio_. Confirm when reserving: _"cochinillo para 4 personas, cuarto cochinillo cada dos"_

### 🥇 Where Segovians eat (menú del día tier — for Emily's own visits)

> The cochinillo trio (Cándido, José María, Duque) run **€30–55/pp**, and Cándido in particular trades on its position under the aqueduct — Spanish forum regulars call it the most tourist-facing of the three, with José María rated the better kitchen. That doesn't make them fake; it makes them an occasion. For an ordinary day in Segovia this is the tier locals use — **weekday menús, €12–15, three courses with bread, drink and dessert**. ⚠️ Weekend menus in the historic centre jump to €25–35, and Sunday is the busiest tourist day: book or eat early.

## 📋 Quick-Reference

- **Lunch timing:** Spain eats late — 13:30 is opening-wave at the classics, 14:30 is peak. The **13:30 sitting** protects the 15:45 Alcázar entry. Allow 2 hours
- **Cochinillo minimum:** usually a _cuarto_ per 2 pax — specify portions when booking
- **Kids/teens:** all three classics are family-friendly — croquetas, jamón plates and patatas exist for anyone pig-averse
- **Budget:** full cochinillo lunch with wine €35–55/pp → **€160–220 for 4**. Worth every cent — it's Segovia's reason
- **No-pork diner:** lechazo (lamb), judiones, sopa castellana, ensalada segoviana — or book El Fogón Sefardí instead
- **Dinner rule:** day-trip only — eat dinner back in Madrid, and keep it light after the pig
