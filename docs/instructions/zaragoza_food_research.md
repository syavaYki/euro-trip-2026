# Zaragoza Food & Drink
## Aug 31 – Sep 1 (1 night) · Euro Trip 2026

> Zaragoza has a strong local food culture — *ternasco* (PDO Aragonese young lamb), *migas* (fried breadcrumbs with chorizo), *borrajas* (a local green), and some of the best cured meats in Spain. The tapas heart is **El Tubo**, a knot of alleys in the old town; Calle El Temple is the more modest second row. **Timing traps for our dates:** we're here Mon Aug 31 (many restaurants' closing day) through Tue Sep 1 (train at 17:48) — and August is staff-holiday month. The plan: El Tubo (verified open Mondays) for Monday, **Casa Lac lunch on Tuesday**.

## 🥩 Must-Try Aragonese Dishes

| Dish | What It Is | Where |
|------|-----------|-------|
| **Ternasco aragonés** ⭐ | Young PDO lamb of Aragón, roasted simply with garlic and olive oil — astonishingly tender | Casa Lac (roast), Doña Casta (croqueta), El Fuelle |
| **Migas aragonesas** | Fried breadcrumbs with chorizo, bacon, garlic, grapes or egg — peasant food elevated to an art form | Casa Lac, traditional restaurants |
| **Trenza de Almudévar** | Braided almond-and-raisin pastry — Zaragoza's signature sweet | Fantoba (the definitive one) |
| **Jamón de Teruel** | Spain's other great PDO ham, from an hour south | Any good bar — ask for a *ración* with your vermouth |
| **Bolaños de La Codoñera** | Small local pickled peppers — perfect with vermouth | Local bodegas — scan the bar at Almau |
