## 🇵🇹 Lisbon

### 🍕 Italian around Cais do Sodré

Came up Tue Aug 25 after we walked out of Time Out Market. The brief was *Italian, good price, not a tourist room*.

| Place | What / why | Where | Price | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **[Lupita Pizzaria](https://maps.google.com/?q=Lupita+Pizzaria+Rua+de+S%C3%A3o+Paulo+79+Lisboa+Portugal)** ⭐ **← our pick** | Naturally-leavened Neapolitan dough, blackened bubbled crust — widely called the best pizzeria in Lisbon | Cais do Sodré · **Rua de São Paulo 79, 1200-427** | €10–14/pizza | Open since Oct 2019. **Walk-in only, expect a queue at peak.** Industrial room, natural wines + craft beer. ~4 min on foot from Time Out Market |
| **[Ristorante Donatella](https://maps.google.com/?q=Trattoria+Donatella+Travessa+do+Carvalho+29+Lisboa+Portugal)** | The one across the street from Time Out that started the search — wood-fired pizza, fresh daily pasta | Cais do Sodré · **Tv. do Carvalho 29, 1200-097** | €15–25/pp | Fine, but Time-Out-adjacent pricing. Lupita is the cheaper, better-regarded version of the same idea |
| **[In Bocca al Lupo](https://maps.google.com/?q=In+Bocca+al+Lupo+Rua+Manuel+Bernardes+5+Lisboa+Portugal)** [🌐](https://www.inboccaallupo.pt/) | Roman-style pizza, stone oven, organic ingredients from Portuguese + Italian producers — the city's first bio pizzeria | Príncipe Real / Mercês · **Rua Manuel Bernardes 5A-B** | €10–16/pizza | Vegetarian, vegan and gluten-free bases. ~12 min uphill from Cais do Sodré. **Nannarella gelato is a short walk away** — natural pairing |
| **[Casanova](https://maps.google.com/?q=Casanova+Cais+da+Pedra+%C3%A0+Bica+do+Sapato+Lisboa+Portugal)** | Long-running riverside pizzeria, thin individual crusts, packed with Lisboetas | Santa Apolónia · **Av. Infante D. Henrique, Cais da Pedra à Bica do Sapato, Armazém B loja 7** | €10–15/pp | **No reservations — you queue.** Riverside terrace. Only worth the trip east if you want the waterfront |

> 💡 **Order note at Lupita:** individual pizzas, so everyone picks their own — closest thing to the Time Out "everyone chooses" appeal without the food-hall mark-up.

### 🏛️ Time Out Market — stall-level notes

We went, looked, and walked out (Tue Aug 25) — too crowded and visitor-priced for what it is. Keeping the notes in case it becomes the fallback on a rushed day.

| Stall | What / why | Price | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Croqueteria** | Croquetes at the counter — shortest queue in the hall, eat standing | €3–5 each | The move when you have ten minutes and no appetite for a sit-down |
| **Manteigaria** (in-market kiosk) | Same natas as the Chiado shop, no detour | ~€1.40 | The one genuinely un-marked-up thing in the building |
| **Henrique Sá Pessoa** | The Alma chef's counter — suckling pig sandwich, octopus. Best quality-per-euro under this roof | €14–18 | The pick if you're eating a real meal here |
| **Marlene Vieira** | Seasonal Portuguese plates from a well-known Lisbon kitchen | ~€15 | Reliable, less queue than Sá Pessoa |
| **Monte Mar** / **Sea Me** | Seafood and fish counters | €15–22 | Fine, but you're paying hall prices for fish you'd get cheaper at a marisqueira |
| **Café de São Bento** | The cult steak sandwich | ~€14 | The one non-obvious order people come back for |

> ⚠️ **Skip:** the burger and sushi counters — generic food at Lisbon-hall prices. And skip anything with a 15-person queue if you're time-boxed.

> 💡 **If you do eat there:** send one person to claim a table first, then everyone orders separately at different stalls and gets buzzed. **Bags in front** — the master file flags this hall as a pickpocket spot in the crush.

### 🥧 Nata & after-dinner, verified on the ground

| Place | What / why | Where | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **[Manteigaria — Chiado](https://maps.google.com/?q=Manteigaria+Rua+do+Loreto+2+Lisboa+Portugal)** [🌐](https://manteigaria.com/en/as-nossas-fabricas/chiado/) ⭐ | The nata stop that works after a late dinner | **Rua do Loreto 2**, Largo de Camões | **Open daily 08:00–24:00** (checked Aug 25, 2026). ~€1.40. **Wait for the bell** — they ring it when a fresh tray leaves the oven. Cinnamon + icing sugar at the counter, eat standing, never take a cold one |

> 💡 **The walk-up from Cais do Sodré:** **Rua do Alecrim**, ~8 min, steep but short — it puts you straight into Largo de Camões with Manteigaria on the corner and Santini five minutes away on Rua do Carmo.

> ⚠️ **Getting home from Camões to the apartment** (Rua da Bempostinha 64, Arroios) is ~2.2 km with a real climb after Avenida — **Bolt it, ~€5–6, 8 min.** Walking it after dinner is a 30-minute uphill mistake.
