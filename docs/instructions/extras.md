# 💡 Extras — Everything We Discussed That Isn't On The Plan

> The bench. Every place that came up in the research, got recommended, got weighed and lost — or was simply never given a slot. Nothing here is booked, nothing here is scheduled, and nothing here needs to happen.
>
> **Use it three ways:** (1) a plan collapses and you need a replacement in the same neighbourhood, (2) an hour opens up and you're already nearby, (3) you're deciding what to come back for. The itinerary stays the source of truth — this is the spare parts drawer.
>
> ⏱️ Ordered by **when you could still use it**: Lisbon's last 36 hours first, then the cities still ahead, then what's already behind us, then the deliberate cuts.

> 💃 **Flamenco in Zaragoza — the honest answer: don't.** There's no tourist tablao. Aragón's own music is the ***jota***, and it lives in *peñas* and one-off theatre bookings. **El Cachirulo** does jota with dinner in full regional costume but it's a 15-min taxi out and leans to weekends — a Monday show is unlikely, **call first**. Better: ask at the **tourist office on Plaza del Pilar** about free summer jota in the plazas, or check the agenda for Teatro Principal / Teatro de las Esquinas / Sala Las Armas. **Do flamenco in Barcelona or Madrid instead.**

**Zaragoza food not slotted:** **Vinos Nicolás** ⭐⭐ (trading since 1912, El Tubo's most famous corner — the third of the three genuinely old houses with Almau 1870 and Casa Lac 1825) · **El Corral de la Delfina** (*casquería* — trotters, callos, breaded ear; the least touristy stop on any crawl) · **La Miguería** ⭐ (*migas aragonesas*, 20+ versions — the regional dish as a whole menu) · **La Republicana** ⭐ (*huevos rotos republicanos*, **menú del día €11.50** — the cheapest proper sit-down in the old town) · Bar Casto (torreznos) · **El Temple pintxos street** (the second row — more locals, fewer English signs; the overflow plan if El Tubo is rammed) · **El Chalet** (top contemporary Aragonese, wants a longer stay) · host's picks **Bar Gallizo** and **El Ángel del Pincho**.
