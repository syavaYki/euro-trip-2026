### Getting Around

- **Metro:** one red **Multi card** (€2.50) + **10-trip Metrobús €7.30** (2026 subsidized price) — shareable, tap once per person. **L3 Legazpi → Sol direct, 12 min** covers most of the plan.
- **Taxis / apps:** Bolt, Uber, Cabify, Free Now all work; €5–12 across the centre; ~€8–10 home from Sol at night. Airport **flat €33**.
- **Cercanías C-4 — Emily's line** ⭐: one Renfe suburban train runs **Atocha → Sol → Nuevos Ministerios → Chamartín → Cantoblanco Universidad**, no changes, every ~5–10 min. **Sol ~20 min · Atocha ~25 · Nuevos Ministerios ~15 · Chamartín ~11.** ⚠️ **The Metro Multi card does NOT work on Cercanías** — separate Renfe ticket, ~€2/pp each way (verify). Both C-4 branches (a/b) call at Cantoblanco. Taxi alternative ~€35–45, 25–30 min.
- **Walking:** flat city. Legazpi → La Latina 15 min · Palacio Real or Prado ~20 min.
- **Weather Sep 1–5:** 26–32°C, dry, nights 16–19°C. **Sunsets ~20:44–20:49.** Shade at siesta hours; evenings outdoors.
- ⚠️ **Reopening week:** Casa Lucio, Posada de la Villa, Bodegas Rosell + others close ALL August and reopen ~Sep 1 — call/book first. Botín, El Brillante, La Bola, Casa Toni run all summer.
