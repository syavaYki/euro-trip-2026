## 🗺️ Section 2 — Hidden Gems & Local Tips

*Sources: YouTube Barcelona expat guides, Reddit r/Barcelona, SpottedByLocals, local expat community*

> **Key local insight (from expats):** Barcelona divides into "upper" (above Diagonal — quieter, safer, local) and "lower" (tourist core). Sant Gervasi, Gràcia and Sarrià are where residents with means actually live. No tourist sights = no tourists = no pickpockets.

## 🚇 Section 8 — Bookings & Practical

> **T-casual €12.55** (10 rides, zone 1, single-person) — buy 4; covers Metro, bus, FGC, Rodalies zone 1, **Montjuïc funicular and Vallvidrera funicular** (both integrated — an earlier note claiming Vallvidrera needs a separate ticket was wrong). The **Montjuïc cable car (€13)** and the Tibidabo Cuca de Llum funicular are separate products. T-familiar €11.50/8 rides is the shareable spare; Hola BCN 96h (€35.60 pp) never breaks even on our ~9–12 rides.

> **Pickpocket zones:** Las Ramblas, Gothic Quarter, Boqueria, L1/L3 platforms at Sagrada Família and Drassanes, the beach. Bags worn front; one adult owns bag-watch on the sand. The upper city (Sant Gervasi, Gràcia, Eixample) is relaxed at all hours.

> **Timed-entry discipline:** Sagrada Família enforces its 15-min window with nominative tickets — **passports in the daypack on Thursday**; Park Güell's 30-min window has no re-entry. Screenshot every code offline: `103719876` (Sagrada), `700328431455` (Park Güell), WJXSCF (AVE out).
