# 🐉 Girona — *Game of Thrones* Filming Locations

> **The short version:** HBO shot **Season 6** in Girona's Barri Vell over roughly **3–18 September 2015**, using the old town as **Braavos**, the cathedral as **King's Landing**, and Sant Pere de Galligants as **Oldtown**. Everything below is within a **~15-minute walk** of everything else, all of it **outdoors and free** except the Arab Baths and the monastery — which are already on the Day 4 plan anyway. **Seven episodes** of S6 carry Girona footage.
>
> 📅 **This is the Saturday Aug 29 day trip.** Nothing here needs its own slot — the route already passes almost every one of these. See *Overview → Full Itinerary → Day 4* for the timed plan.

## ⏱️ Section 2 — If you only have 20 minutes

The day is already full. **These three, in this order, are the whole thing:**

1. **Cathedral steps** (#8) — the Great Sept. From the bottom of the square, looking up. *Already at 12:35.*
2. **Escales de Sant Martí** (#11) — Arya's fall. Two minutes' walk from the cathedral. *Already at 16:00.*
3. **Sant Pere de Galligants** (#1) — the Citadel. *Already at 11:30.*

> 💡 **Everything else is free and takes zero extra time** — #2, #4, #5, #6, #7, #9, #10 and #12 all sit *on* the existing walking line. The only genuine detour is the **Arab Baths** (#3), ~30–40 min and ~€3, and that one is a good hot-afternoon call on its own merits.

## 📌 Section 5 — Practical Notes

- **Nothing is ticketed as a "GoT site."** No plaques, no ropes, no queue. The cathedral steps and the Sant Martí steps are public streets, free at any hour.
- **Best light:** the cathedral staircase faces roughly **west-southwest** — it's flat and washed-out at midday, and best **late afternoon**. If the photo matters more than the plan, shoot it on the way *back* from lunch rather than at 12:35.
- ⚠️ **August heat.** Girona runs **32–34 °C**, hotter than Barcelona. #5 (Jardins de la Francesa) and #3 (Arab Baths) are the two genuinely cool places on this list — use them.
- **Guided GoT tours** run ~€20–25 pp, 1.5–2 h, from Plaça de la Independència. **Skip it** — the sites are 400 m apart and this page is the tour.
- 🎧 The **cathedral's own €7.50 audio guide** (included) covers the filming, so you get the official version of the story without paying for a separate tour.
- **Teenager angle:** this is the single best "they'll actually care" thread of the Barcelona block. Load the clips above on a phone **before leaving the flat** — Girona's old town has patchy signal in the stone lanes.
