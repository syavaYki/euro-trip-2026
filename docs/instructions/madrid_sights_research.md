## ⚰️ Historic Cemeteries (family priority)

> Both great Victorian necropolises are free, silent, and unvisited. Unscheduled this trip — they need a half-day the itinerary doesn't have; keep for a return or swap into a collapsed plan.

| Name | Why go | Cost | Hours | Tip |
|------|--------|------|-------|-----|
| **Cementerio de la Almudena** [📍](https://maps.google.com/?q=Cementerio+de+la+Almudena+Madrid+Spain) ⭐ | **Spain's largest cemetery** — ~5 million graves, more than Madrid's living population; pantheon avenues in every style from Egyptian Revival to Art Nouveau | Free | Daily ~08:00–18:30+ | Metro La Almudena (L2); aim for Avenida Principal's pantheons and the Egyptian pyramid of the Marqueses de Hinojares |
| **Sacramental de San Isidro** [📍](https://maps.google.com/?q=Cementerio+Sacramental+de+San+Isidro+Madrid+Spain) | Madrid's Père-Lachaise (1811) — Romantic mausoleums; Goya's first grave | Free | Daily ~08:00–18:30+ | 20-min walk over the river from La Latina; the Patio de la Purísima is the beautiful core |
| **Panteón de los Hombres Ilustres** [📍](https://maps.google.com/?q=Pante%C3%B3n+de+los+Hombres+Ilustres+Madrid+Spain) | Tiny neo-Byzantine pantheon of 19th-c prime ministers — six spectacular marble tombs, zero visitors | Free | Tue–Sat 10–13:30 + 16–18:30, Sun 10–15, closed Mon | 5 min from Atocha — the only cemetery stop that fits a spare 30 min on the museum axis |

> ⚠️ **Cantoblanco has nothing around it.** The campus sits alone in open scrub off the M-607: no town, no old quarter, no square. Don't plan to "wander nearby." The three honest options while waiting are (1) ride the C-4 back into the city — it's 20–25 min to Atocha or Sol, which is what Day 3 does; (2) **Alcobendas**, 10 min by taxi (~€12) — **Centro de Arte Alcobendas** is free with a strong photography collection (Tue–Sat, verify), Parque de Andalucía has a lake, and Moraleja Green / Plaza Norte 2 are air-conditioned malls if it's 32 °C; (3) stay on campus — the UAM **Plaza Mayor**, its cafeterías, the library and the bookshop, which is genuinely worth seeing once since she'll live there.
