## 🥪 The bifana shortlist

Became the pick when Afonso went dark |
| **[Café Beira Gare](https://maps.google.com/?q=Beira+Gare+Pra%C3%A7a+Dom+Jo%C3%A3o+da+C%C3%A2mara+4+Lisboa)** | More pronounced buttery-garlic sauce; old-school room | Pç. Dom João da Câmara 4, opposite Rossio station | **Mon–Sat 11:30–22:00, closed Sundays** |
| ~~**[As Bifanas do Afonso](https://maps.google.com/?q=As+Bifanas+do+Afonso+Rua+da+Madalena+146+Lisboa)**~~ ⚠️ | *Was* the pick — a hole in the wall making essentially one thing, **~€2 a sandwich**, the locals' choice | R. da Madalena 146 | 🔴 **Google showed it TEMPORARILY CLOSED on 25 Aug 2026.** No closure reported anywhere online — most likely the August family holiday. **Re-check the live listing before walking over**; if it's back, it's still the best of them (Mon–Fri 07:30–19:30, Sat to 14:00, Sun shut) |
| **[O Trevo](https://maps.google.com/?q=O+Trevo+Pra%C3%A7a+Lu%C3%ADs+de+Cam%C3%B5es+Lisboa)** | The Anthony Bourdain one — most famous bifana counter in the world. The soul of it is the back counter where locals stand | Praça Luís de Camões, Chiado | Wrong side of town if you're coming down from Alfama |

> ⚠️ **Lesson worth keeping: check Google's live "Open now" before crossing town for any small Lisbon room**, especially in August. Printed hours and travel guides lag by months; the owner's own listing does not.

> 💡 **Order like a local:** *"uma bifana e uma imperial."* Mustard and piri-piri are on the counter — say yes.

## 🚌 Hard-won logistics notes

> The things that cost us time to find out. Worth keeping.

- **Bus 434 in Sintra is a ONE-WAY loop.** Station → Sintra Vila → Castelo dos Mouros → Pena → **straight back down to the station**. It does **not** stop at the Moorish Castle on the way down, so you cannot ride Pena → Moors. Taxi, tuk-tuk or the 10 min walk down the road.
- **Pena's park transfer ticket (€4.50) is a RETURN** — hop-on-hop-off, valid 365 days, covers up *and* down. Parques de Sintra's own FAQ.
- **Pena's timed slot is the PALACE door, not the park gate** — allow ~30 min between them. Early arrivals are refused as firmly as late ones.
- **Bus 101 (Cacilhas ↔ Cristo Rei)** — last departure around **18:17**, useless for an evening descent. Bolt back instead.
- **Cristo Rei closes 19:00 (Apr–Sep), last ascent 18:40** — so there is no sunset from the platform, ever. Sunset belongs to the Cacilhas dock.
- **MEO Blueticket has two separate sites.** Monument vouchers bought from *Museus e Monumentos* are redeemed at **[mmp.bymeoblueticket.pt](https://mmp.bymeoblueticket.pt/en)** (the "Reschedule" form on the homepage), **not** blueticket.meo.pt — whose account tabs answer *"You don't have tickets"* for un-redeemed vouchers.
- **A Blueticket "voucher" is not a ticket.** Open-dated, says *"THIS VOUCHER DOES NOT GRANT ENTRY"*, and must be scheduled into a slot before the gate will scan anything.
- **Train Belém ↔ Cais do Sodré** — ~7 min, ~€1.60pp on zapping, and Cais do Sodré is the terminus so you can't overshoot. **Always validate before boarding** — no barriers at Belém, but inspectors do check.
- **Portuguese state museums** often open **free before 14:00 on Sundays** — worth asking at the desk, though it may be residents-only.

## 🏰 Sintra leftovers

- **[Vila Sassetti trail](https://maps.google.com/?q=Vila+Sassetti+Sintra+Portugal)** — the signposted zigzag footpath from the Moorish Castle down through woodland and villa gardens to Volta do Duche. ~35 min, the best walking in Sintra. ⚠️ Uneven stone and loose gravel — not in the wet.
- **[Chalet da Condessa d'Edla](https://maps.google.com/?q=Chalet+da+Condessa+d%27Edla+Sintra+Portugal)** — inside Pena park, the place to spend a spare 20 minutes if you're early for the palace slot.
- **[Apeadeiro](https://maps.google.com/?q=Restaurante+Apeadeiro+Sintra+Portugal)** ⭐⭐ — the Sintra locals' room, 46 years old, big azulejo hall by the town hall. €12–20/pp, and it's on the way to the station.
- **[A Tasca do Manel](https://maps.google.com/?q=Tasca+do+Manel+Sintra+Portugal)** — bread + soup + steak + drink + coffee ~**€8**.
- **[Cantinho de São Pedro](https://maps.google.com/?q=Cantinho+de+S%C3%A3o+Pedro+Sintra+Portugal)** — family-run, codfish gratin and pork cheeks, €15–25/pp. Best food of the three, but ~25 min walk / 6 min Bolt.
- **[Incomum](https://maps.google.com/?q=Incomum+Sintra+Portugal)** — the one that actually opens at night, near the station. Book ahead.
- **[Sintra Mitos e Lendas](https://maps.google.com/?q=Sintra+Mitos+e+Lendas)** — ~€12, 17 multimedia rooms of local legends, daily 10:00–18:30. The air-conditioned cool-down.
- ⚠️ **Sintra kitchens shut ~16:00 across the board** and most don't reopen until ~19:00. Monday evening is close to dead.

## 🕍 Belém leftovers

- **[Museu Nacional dos Coches](https://maps.google.com/?q=Museu+Nacional+dos+Coches+Lisbon+Portugal)** — world's finest royal carriages, gilded past all reason, air-conditioned. **Tue–Sun 10:00–18:30, closed Mondays.** ~€8 main hall / ~€15 combined with the old Picadeiro; 13–24 half price, under 12 free.
- **[Museu de Arte Popular](https://maps.google.com/?q=Museu+de+Arte+Popular+Lisboa+Portugal)** — on the waterfront between the Padrão and the Tower. The **Jerónimos ticket carries 5% off its shop**.
- **[O Prado](https://maps.google.com/?q=O+Prado+Rua+da+Junqueira+472+Lisboa+Portugal)** ⭐⭐ — the locals' tasca one street back from the monument row. €10–15/pp, and the tour buses never turn the corner.
- **[Afonso dos Leitões](https://maps.google.com/?q=Afonso+dos+Leit%C3%B5es+Rua+da+Junqueira+486+Lisboa+Portugal)** — roast suckling-pig sandwich with peppery gravy, eaten standing. €6–10/pp.
- **[Pão Pão Queijo Queijo](https://maps.google.com/?q=P%C3%A3o+P%C3%A3o+Queijo+Queijo+Rua+de+Bel%C3%A9m+126+Lisboa+Portugal)** — genuinely cheap on the tourist strip, €6–9, and the queue moves.
- **Riverside kiosks, [Jardim de Belém](https://maps.google.com/?q=Jardim+de+Bel%C3%A9m+Lisboa+Portugal)** — *prego* and a beer in the shade, kids roam the lawn.
